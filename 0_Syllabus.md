### <a id="CourseDescription"></a> Course Description
The course focuses on applications of deep neural networks in common engineering tasks such as prediction, time-series forecasting, and monitoring, typically encountered in a range of industries including health, manufacturing, energy, and supply chain. We will learn:
1.	Foundational models, including Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs), Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), and Transformers, that learn from data patterns. 
2.	Physics-based models, including Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINN), and Neural Ordinary Differential Equations (NeuralODEs) that combine neural networks with mathematical rigor of differential equations for time-series data. 
3.	Explainable Machine Learning (XAI) techniques for explainability, which are relevant in safety-critical applications, and to make models more transparent and understandable to stakeholders in engineering contexts. 
4.	Basics of agentic AI workflows that build on these foundational models. 

Assignments in the 600-level class will have a methodological component that requires a deeper understanding of the techniques studied, beyond their computational implementation.
